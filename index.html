Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding pros and getting work done.

How does Revise work?
Tell us what you need done, and we'll match you with verified local contractors in your area. Compare profiles, ratings, and estimates, then message or book the pro that's the best fit — all in one place.
Is it free for homeowners to use?
Yes. Browsing contractors, requesting estimates, and messaging pros is always free for homeowners. You only pay the contractor directly for the work you book.
How are contractors verified?
Every contractor on Revise submits identity verification and relevant licensing or insurance information before their profile goes live. Verified pros display a badge on their profile so you know who you're hiring.
What if I'm not happy with the work?
Reach out to the contractor directly first through in-app chat to resolve the issue. If that doesn't work, contact our support team and we'll help mediate a resolution.
Can I request emergency help?
Yes — our Emergency Help section connects you with available pros for urgent issues like burst pipes, gas leaks, and power outages, with typical response times under 45 minutes.
How do I sign up as a contractor?
Select "Join as a Pro" from the sign-up screen, complete your business profile, and submit verification documents. Most applications are reviewed within a few business days.
What does it cost to join as a contractor?
Creating a profile is free. Revise takes a small service fee only on jobs booked through the platform — there are no upfront costs or monthly subscriptions to get started.
How do I get more leads?
Complete your profile with photos of past work, respond quickly to messages, and keep your ratings up — profiles with more detail and faster response times are shown higher in search results.
How do payments work?
Homeowners and contractors agree on pricing directly, and payment can be made through the app or arranged separately, depending on the contractor's preference. Estimate requests are always free.
Are there hidden fees?
No. Revise is built around transparent pricing — any service fee is shown clearly before you confirm a booking, with no surprise charges added afterward.
How do I update my profile or account info?
Go to your account menu and select Edit Profile to update your contact details, photo, and preferences at any time.
How do I delete my account?
Contact our support team at revise@outlook.com and we'll process your account deletion request, along with removal of your personal data in line with our Privacy Policy.

Safety & Trust

How we help keep every job on Revise safe, transparent, and reliable.

Identity verification

Every contractor confirms their identity before their profile can go live on Revise, so you know exactly who you're inviting into your home.

License & insurance checks

Where required by trade and location, contractors provide licensing and insurance documentation, which is reviewed as part of the verification process.

Verified reviews

Ratings and reviews are only left by homeowners who booked a job through Revise, keeping feedback honest and tied to real work.

In-app messaging

Chat with contractors directly through Revise so there's a clear, timestamped record of everything discussed before and during a job.

Transparent pricing

Estimates and any service fees are shown clearly before you confirm a booking — no hidden charges added after the fact.

Report a concern

If something feels off about a profile, message, or job, you can report it to our support team any time and we'll investigate promptly.

Tips for a safe experience

  • Always communicate and confirm job details through the Revise app before work begins.
  • Check a contractor's verification badge, rating, and reviews before booking.
  • Get an estimate in writing before agreeing to any job.
  • Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, pause the job and contact support.
  • Never share sensitive financial information like bank logins or card numbers over chat.

Privacy Policy

How Revise collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

On this page

1. Information we collect 2. How we use your information 3. How we share information 4. Data retention 5. Your choices & rights 6. Cookies & tracking 7. Security 8. Children's privacy 9. Accessibility 10. Changes to this policy 11. Contact us

This Privacy Policy explains how Revise ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our website and app (the "Service"). By using Revise, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you create an account, request an estimate, or book a job, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, service address, and payment-related details. Contractors additionally provide business information, licensing and insurance documentation, and photos of past work.

Information collected automatically

We automatically collect certain data when you use the Service, including device information, IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We also use your device's approximate location — derived from your IP address or the postal code you enter — to help match you with nearby pros.

Precise location (with your permission)

Some features — like the "Use my location" search option and the homepage list of pros near you — ask your browser for your device's precise GPS/network location via its Geolocation API. Your browser will always show its own permission prompt before this happens, and we only receive your coordinates if you approve that prompt. We use precise location solely to sort and match you with nearby contractors during that session; we don't store your precise coordinates as part of your account profile, and you can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your browser's site settings without losing access to the rest of the Service (search still works with a typed postal code instead).

Information from messages and reviews

Messages sent through in-app chat, along with ratings and reviews you submit, are stored as part of your account activity.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and connect homeowners with contractors
  • To process estimate requests, bookings, and payments
  • To verify contractor identity, licensing, and insurance
  • To send booking updates, service notifications, and customer support responses
  • To improve the Service, including search matching and fraud prevention
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. How we share information

We share limited information between homeowners and contractors as needed to facilitate a job — for example, your name, service address, and job details are shared with a contractor once you request an estimate or booking.

We may also share information with service providers who help us operate the Service (such as payment processors and hosting providers), and when required by law, regulation, or legal process. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, and comply with our legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account at any time, as described below.

5. Your choices & rights

You can access and update most of your information directly in your account settings. Depending on your location, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and associated personal information, subject to legal or contractual retention needs
  • Portability — request your information in a portable format
  • Objection / opt-out — object to certain processing, including marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at revise@outlook.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity first.

6. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect certain features. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising, and we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. Payment card details are handled directly by Stripe, our payment processor — Revise never stores your full card number. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

Revise is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Accessibility

We're committed to making Revise usable by everyone. See our Accessibility Statement for details on our approach and how to report an issue.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through additional notice within the Service.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at revise@outlook.com.

Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making Revise usable by everyone.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

On this page

1. Our commitment 2. Standards we follow 3. Accessibility features 4. Known limitations 5. Reporting an issue

1. Our commitment

Revise is a non-profit built to connect homeowners with local contractors, and that mission only works if the platform is usable by everyone — including people who use assistive technology like screen readers, switch devices, or voice control, and people navigating with a keyboard alone. We're working to make Revise accessible to as many people as possible, and we treat accessibility as an ongoing responsibility, not a one-time checklist.

2. Standards we follow

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the widely-used standard for web accessibility. This includes guidance on color contrast, text alternatives for non-text content, keyboard operability, and helping people navigate, find content, and understand where they are.

3. Accessibility features

  • Semantic HTML structure and landmarks to support screen reader navigation
  • Keyboard-operable interactive elements, including forms, buttons, and modals
  • Text alternatives for informative icons and images
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background across the site
  • Resizable text that doesn't break layout when browser zoom is increased
  • Visible focus indicators so keyboard users can track where they are on the page

4. Known limitations

Despite our best efforts, some parts of Revise may not yet be fully accessible — for example, some map-based content (like the contractor search map) is inherently visual, and we're working on stronger non-visual alternatives for it. Third-party content, such as Stripe's checkout flow, is governed by that provider's own accessibility practices. We're actively working through our backlog to close these gaps.

5. Reporting an issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier while using Revise, please let us know — specific details (the page, what you were trying to do, and your browser or assistive technology) help us fix it faster. Contact us at revise@outlook.com and we'll work to address it and get back to you.

Our story

Ever since I was a kid, I watched my father struggle with the same problem so many contractors face: work that comes in waves. Some weeks brought a flood of jobs — constant calls, packed schedules, no time to breathe. Other weeks, the phone barely rang. And when he did need new clients, his only real options were word of mouth and the occasional ad, both slow and unpredictable. Revise exists to fix that — giving contractors like my father a steady, reliable stream of leads, and giving homeowners an easy way to find them, all for free.

So we built Revise to fix both sides of that problem: a place where verified contractors can show their real work and reviews, and where homeowners can book with confidence, see fair pricing upfront, and get help fast when something goes wrong.

Our mission

We're building the most trusted way to get home work done — for homeowners who want it done right, and for contractors who deserve to be found for great work.
